Journal article / June 2026

Philippine Eagle Release Sites in Leyte

Identified spatially explicit and secure release sites for reintroducing critically endangered Philippine Eagles in Leyte. Published in the Philippine Journal of Science, 155(3), 631-641.

My part. Coordinated expert consultations with aviary experts and the Philippine Eagle Foundation, including serving as live correspondent to its director of operations. Translated expert recommendations into GIS criteria, then wrote and interpreted the findings.

Methods
  • Converted ecological criteria (slope, powerline buffers, settlement proximity) into spatial constraints.
  • Processed DEMs, Human Footprint Index, and Key Biodiversity Areas.
  • Applied GIS multi-criteria decision-making with Boolean overlays to filter out high-hazard zones.

Outcome. About 10,320 ha of suitable habitat identified, with over 9,500 ha classed as highly secure release zones away from human infrastructure.

#QGIS #MCDM #Conservation

Conference paper / February 2026

Dasymetric Population Mapping for Urban Settlements

Generated block-level population density maps for formal and informal settlements in Marikina City from satellite imagery. Published in ISPRS Annals, Vol. X-5/W4-2025 (PhilGEOS 2025), pp. 401-408.

My part. Collected and prepared the ancillary data: WorldPop density, PSA census and city population figures, and census blocks. Clipped OpenStreetMap layers and converted river and building vectors into distance rasters for land cover change modeling.

Methods
  • Classified urban land cover from Sentinel-2 imagery by analyzing irregular roof patterns.
  • Compared Random Forest, OBIA, and hybrid OBIA-RF models.
  • Downscaled barangay census data into 30 m grid populations based on settlement patterns.

Outcome. 72.19% classification accuracy for informal settlements, revealing high-density population clusters that standard census maps hide.

#QGIS #GoogleEarthEngine #RandomForest

Undergraduate thesis / Sept 2025 to May 2026

Urban Housing Rent Prediction, Barangay Manggahan

Mapped unrecorded building ages to predict fine-scale rental prices in a mixed-income barangay in Pasig City, using a gradient boosting approach.

My part. Developed a Python algorithm that estimates building construction and completion years from Landsat time series, trained and validated on manually traced construction histories. A rate-based approach outperformed a magnitude-based one. Produced the study maps and ran Moran's I on rent and building age clustering.

Methods
  • Built a custom Python geocoder with the Google Places API to map web-scraped rental listings.
  • Extracted building ages with the LandTrendr algorithm on Landsat time series in Google Earth Engine.
  • Trained an XGBoost model with SHAP analysis to find spatial price drivers.

Outcome. Neighborhood spatial lag drives rent by P60/sqm, outperforming individual building age and quality.

#Python #XGBoost #SHAP #ArcGIS #EarthEngine

Web GIS / Participatory SDSS

Vendor Relocation SDSS (Kabuhayan)

A participatory web-GIS tool for negotiating mutually acceptable street vendor relocation sites in Manila.

Methods
  • Built a React/Leaflet mapping app backed by PostgreSQL/PostGIS to manage stakeholder inputs and layers such as flood risk and traffic.

Outcome. A working web-map prototype that balances vendor livelihood needs with city traffic ordinances.

Internship project / July 2025

Sediment Plumes in Bulacan and Valenzuela

Mapped sediment plumes and muddy floodwaters to identify critical areas needing erosion control near the New Manila International Airport.

Methods
  • Processed high-resolution PlanetScope imagery in QGIS at Geospectrum.
  • Used the Flood Mud Index on red and blue bands instead of NDTI to better separate turbid floodwater from dry land.

Outcome. High-turbidity sediment plumes delineated, giving usable geospatial intelligence for targeted erosion control in fast-developing coastal zones.
